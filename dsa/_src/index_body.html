How to use each page

Learning a topic here works like learning to drive. First someone tells you what a clutch is in everyday terms (the real-life example). Then you watch an instructor do it (the animation). Then you drive a quiet street with them beside you (the worked problem), and finally you take the test (the quiz and the LeetCode list).

  1. Read the real-life example and the idea. Make sure you could explain it to a friend.
  2. Play each animation step by step. Pause and predict the next step before you click.
  3. Type every pattern template from memory in Colab or LeetCode until you can write it without looking.
  4. Read the deep analysis, then close the page and solve that problem yourself.
  5. Take the quiz, Easy first. Aim for 8 out of 10 before moving on.
  6. Work the problem list and tick each one off. Your progress is saved in this browser and shown below.
