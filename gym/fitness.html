00 Overview & Honest Expectations

Losing 15 lbs (200 → 185) in 8 weeks works out to about 1.9 lbs/week — an aggressive rate. The generally recommended safe, sustainable pace is 1-1.5 lb/week, especially while lifting weights, so you lose mostly fat and not muscle. This plan targets a realistic ~1-1.2 lb/week rate, landing you around 190-191 lbs by the end of week 8. If you're still a few pounds from 185 at that point, repeat weeks 7-8 for another 2-4 weeks.

Calorie targets are estimated from your stats (Mifflin-St Jeor formula) with a moderate-active multiplier, adjusted for a fat-loss deficit. They're a well-informed starting point, not an exact science — adjust up or down by 100-150 kcal after week 2 based on how the scale and your energy are trending.

04 Phase 1 — Weeks 1-2: Foundation

Goal: groove movement patterns, build a running base, and let your body adapt to a new routine without excessive soreness or burnout. Effort: moderate (leave 2-3 reps in reserve on lifts).

  • After swim: 3-5 min easy jog or dynamic stretches to re-warm-up
  • 20 minutes continuous jog, conversational-but-purposeful pace (Zone 2-3) — may feel tougher on swim fatigue, that's normal
  • 5 min cool-down walk/jog + mobility: hip flexor stretch, couch stretch, ankle circles, foam rolling
  • Snack tip: small carb snack (banana, dates, crackers) between swim and run

  • 6+ miles at an easy, conversational pace (Zone 2) — time on your feet, not speed
  • Walk the first and last 5 minutes as warm-up/cool-down if needed
  • Optional: 1 easy round of core afterward (Plank 30s, Leg Raise 10) — skip if tired

Sunday — Rest

Easy 20-30 min walk, stretch, or full rest. If you want to keep swimming in the mix, an easy 15-20 min swim works well here too — keep it light, it's still a recovery day.

05 Phase 2 — Weeks 3-4: Build

Goal: add volume and intensity now that movement patterns feel solid. Increase strength loads 5-10% from Phase 1 where you had 2+ reps in reserve. Sprint efforts move to 85-90%.

  • 10 min warm-up jog + dynamic stretches
  • 8 x 100m sprints at 85-90% effort, walk-back recovery (slightly shorter rest than Phase 1)
  • 10 min cool-down + stretching

After swim, re-warm-up 3-5 min, then increase to 25-28 minutes continuous at the same conversational-effort pace, plus mobility work.

Saturday — Long Run (Progression)

  • 6-7 miles at an easy-to-moderate pace, slightly more purposeful than Phase 1 but still conversational
  • Optional: pick up the pace slightly for the final 5 minutes if feeling strong

06 Phase 3 — Weeks 5-6: Intensity

Goal: heavier strength work at lower reps for continued muscle retention/strength, and faster, harder sprint work for conditioning and calorie burn. This is the most demanding phase — sleep and protein intake matter most here.

  • 10 min warm-up
  • 10 x 100m sprints at 90% effort, walk-back recovery
  • Once per week this phase (Tuesday), add 4 x 20-second hill sprints at the end if a hill is available
  • 10 min cool-down

Thursday — Swim Class (6:00-6:30 PM) + Tempo Run

After swim, re-warm-up 3-5 min, then 30 minutes continuous, pace slightly faster than Phase 2 while still conversational.

Saturday — Long Run (Peak)

  • 7 miles, moderate effort throughout — still sustainable, but noticeably more purposeful than Phase 1-2
  • Optional: last mile at a slightly faster tempo effort if energy allows

07 Phase 4 — Weeks 7-8: Peak & Taper

Week 7 is your hardest week. Week 8 is a planned taper (reduce volume ~30-40%) so you finish the block feeling strong and recovered rather than run down — this also protects against overtraining and gives your body a chance to show the full result of 7 weeks of work.

  • 10 min cool-down

Week 7 — Thursday Swim Class (6:00-6:30 PM) + Tempo Run

After swim, re-warm-up 3-5 min, then 30-35 minutes continuous at Phase 3 effort — hold steady, this is your recovery day between hard sprint/lift/long-run sessions.

Week 7 — Saturday Long Run (Peak)

  • 7-8 miles at moderate effort — your longest run of the program
  • Optional: last 1-2 miles at a faster tempo effort if feeling strong

  • Thursday: Swim class + 20 minutes easy continuous jogging — taper volume down like everything else this week
  • Saturday long run (taper): keep it at 6 miles (baseline), but at an easy, relaxed pace — this week is about recovery, not effort
  • Friday of week 8 (optional): time yourself over 100m or a 1-mile run to measure speed/conditioning gains since week 1
End of Week 8: re-weigh yourself under the same conditions as day 1, and compare. If you're close to 185 but not quite there, repeat the Week 7-8 template (Phase 3 loads, taper every 4th week) until you hit goal.

08 Nutrition Guidelines

Protein (25-40g/meal, 4-5 meals/day)

Chicken breast, lean beef/turkey, white fish, salmon, eggs/egg whites, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, whey or plant protein powder, tofu/tempeh, legumes.

Carbs (load around Tue & Sat)

Rice, oats, potatoes/sweet potatoes, whole-grain bread/pasta, fruit, beans, quinoa.

Fats

Olive oil, avocado, nuts/nut butter, egg yolks, fatty fish.

Practical Tips

  • Weigh yourself once a week, same morning, same conditions — day-to-day swings are mostly water.
  • Hit your protein target every day even more consistently than the exact calorie number.
  • Drink water throughout the day; aim for at least half your bodyweight (lbs) in ounces.
  • Sleep 7-9 hours — recovery drives both fat loss and strength/speed gains.
  • If a training day leaves you unusually hungry or flat, add 100-150 kcal from protein/carbs rather than skipping food.
